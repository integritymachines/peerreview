OBJECTIVE

Economical scientific prescreening before full review. Identify consequential flaws, assess scientific contribution, test headline claims and precedence over alternatives.

INPUT (read only)

#D, #Da, #Db…: Paper and supplements
#C: Criteria & bookshelf
review.txt: Governing review workflow

OUTPUT

#S0: Prescan scorecard

TASKS

Execute one abbreviated, simulated review under review.txt standards.

Use 2–3 contrasting named scholarly traditions.

No independent agent runs, repeated rounds, huddles, callbacks, or protocol.

Apply relevant criteria, including I/Q. Prioritize fatal/critical flaws, claim validity, alternative explanations, novelty and significance.

Selective literature checks only where consequential. No mandatory literature assignments or exhaustive searches.

Produce compact #S0: two-sentence summary, consequential flags with color/severity/visibility, pinpoint evidence, claim impact/precedence, repair effort.

Distinguish verified flaws from suspicions.

AUTHORITY

review.txt governs scientific standards, criteria, traditions and flag definitions.

prescan.txt overrides execution and reporting only. Findings provisional; no contamination of independent full-review runs.

NOTES

No #P. No housekeeping. Avoid reporting inconsequential flaws.
