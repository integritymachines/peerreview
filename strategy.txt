PBPR — Peer Review — Strategy
v0.1 | 2026-11-28 | GPT-6.2
Status: A APPROVED.

AMBITION
Establish a repeatable, criteria-driven architecture for rigorous adversarial academic review, constructive pre-submission revision and editorial assessment.

RATIONALE
Claim survival and comparative precedence are separate tests. Diverse independent searches followed by adjudication can expose weaknesses missed by a single review.

SCOPE
Review workflow, criteria and bookshelves, scorecards, constructive preview and editorial reports. Catalogue scope may be discipline-specific. Review content rather than irrelevant ancillary information.

APPROACH
Route by epistemic anchor and competencies; perform independent preliminary runs with flag-blind reviewers; adjudicate material findings and alternatives in a deciding run.

ACTORS, ROLES, AUTHORITIES (ARA)
A governs project intent. Within review: neutral system routes; assigning editor scopes; reviewers search; huddle adjudicates; deciding editor controls and consolidates. Human editorial decisions remain with their actual authority.

STOCKS, FLOWS, TRANSFORMATIONS (SFT)
Stocks: criteria, bookshelves, submissions, scorecards and protocols. Flows: manuscript to independent preliminary reviews to deciding run to review outputs. Transformations: routing, adversarial search, substantiation, deduplication and adjudication.

PRINCIPLES
Search broadly; flag carefully. Headline claim no stronger than surviving claim; require precedence over viable alternatives. Preserve disagreements and distinguish simulated from independent review.

CONSTRAINTS
Confidentiality and run isolation where required; cite evidence accurately; no fabricated references or human editorial authority.

ENDPOINT
A documented, reusable and demonstrably rigorous review protocol and catalogue construction method. Ongoing application and improvement; validation thresholds and journal adoption goals remain unspecified.
