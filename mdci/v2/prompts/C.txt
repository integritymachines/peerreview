Framework vocabulary (not mutually exclusive schools):
- potential_outcomes: Rubin/Neyman treatment- or regime-indexed counterfactual outcomes and assignment/identification restrictions. This does not inherently require randomization or no unmeasured confounding.
- structural_causal_model: Pearl-style causal assignments with exogenous variables and intervention/counterfactual semantics, possibly graphical. Optimization and equilibrium are assumptions, not physical laws. This framework can use potential outcomes and need not be a parametric Heckman correction.
- mechanistic_dynamical: an explicit mechanistic state-evolution/intervention model grounded in relevant physical or biological processes; distinguish empirically supported constraints from assumed compartment equations and numerical calibration.
