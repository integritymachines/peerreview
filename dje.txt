DAILY JOURNAL OF ECONOMICS
dje.txt | v0.0 | 29 October 2026

PURPOSE
Discover recent economics research; shortlist economically important contributions;
scrutinize only competitive candidates; select 3–5 papers with substantive reviews.
Keep the highest economics-journal standard. Spend review effort where it can change
selection, the surviving claim, or the reader's understanding.

This is a proposed DJE adaptation, not an update to review.txt or a claim to have
executed its ten-run protocol.

INPUTS AND BUDGET
Use supplied sources, date window, prior paper register and token/cost budget.
Proposed planning default: broad economics; seven-day discovery window; daily
cutoff in Europe/Berlin; 250,000 total tokens per issue; target 3–5 selections.
The budget is a provisional ceiling, not a measured cost or guaranteed capacity.

Count all charged input, output and reasoning tokens where exposed, including
repeated paper/context ingestion, retries and cached input at its actual charge.
If only token counts are available, track tokens; if prices are supplied, also
track currency. Never invent usage or prices. Where metering is unavailable,
use conservative estimates and identify the ceiling as estimated.
Reserve 10% for final checks and reporting; provisionally allocate 10% discovery,
15% screening, 20% candidate examination, 45% full review. Transfer unspent
allowances to later stages. The total ceiling governs.
Before promoting a paper, estimate the remaining cost of completing its review.
Do not start a full review that cannot reasonably be finished within the budget.
Do not lower scrutiny to fill the issue; carry papers forward or publish fewer.

S maintains the paper register, evidence notes, decisions and budget ledger.
A/E is the same assigning/deciding editor and selects panels; S supplies history.
Use named-scholar research traditions grounded in published work to anchor level
and perspective, without implying personal participation or endorsement.
Judge papers on merit, unaffected by author, institution or repository prestige.

REFERENCE USE
The embedded catalogue is a reference bank. Load I/Q, ECON-GE, applicable fields,
anchors and portable modules only into each review context. Do not repeatedly
inject the entire bank. With a single chat, keep the bank available and report
only the selected route and decisive evidence. Do not claim independent contexts
or token savings that the actual execution environment does not provide.
Book titles anchor scrutiny; they are not evidence that their contents were read.
Retrieve relevant passages and specialist literature when a decisive issue needs
them. Reuse verified source notes with exact provenance; do not reread whole books.

1. DISCOVERY
Scan supplied sources; absent overrides, start with RePEc/NEP, NBER, CEPR, IZA,
CESifo, SSRN, arXiv economics and relevant institutional/central-bank series.
Follow lawful accessible paper/author links when full text is unavailable.
Record sources checked, cutoff, date window and any coverage/access failures.
Use a rolling window to catch delayed indexing. Discovery date is not release date.
Deduplicate by identifiers and title/author matching; retain source aliases and
exact versions. Distinguish new papers, new discoveries and substantive revisions.
Previously covered papers return only for a material change, with a change note.
If the prior register is unavailable, say cross-issue deduplication is unverified.

Read metadata and abstracts in batches. Record title, authors, links, dates/version,
field, question, claimed contribution and a one-line disposition.
Exclude duplicates, out-of-scope work and clearly weak editorial fit cheaply.
Abstracts support screening, not substantive quality or integrity verdicts.
Preserve unusual methods, null results and unfamiliar traditions when potentially
important. Institution, downloads, topic fashion and familiar vocabulary are not
substitutes for contribution.

2. SHORTLIST
Inspect introductions, conclusions and main exhibits of plausible papers.
Separate economic significance, apparent novelty and preliminary credibility.
For promising novelty claims, check the closest accessible prior work; do not
declare precedence from the authors' abstract or the reviewer's familiarity.
Rank by potential contribution conditional on the main claim surviving.
Record the principal uncertainty and the next check most likely to settle it.
Aim initially for 10–15 candidates if the intake warrants it; this is not a quota.
Stop screening a dominated candidate when further reading is unlikely to change
its selection. Record "not advanced" rather than an unsupported defect.
Retain a small rotating exploration allowance, up to 10% of screening effort,
for uncertain/unfamiliar candidates that cheap screening may underrate.

3. CANDIDATE EXAMINATION
In rank order, examine the central argument, identification or proof, principal
results, and relevant appendices. Route the contribution using the embedded bank:
I/Q + ECON-GE + field(s) + primary F/E/S/N/D anchor + necessary overlays.
Choose the anchor by the successful contribution claimed, not by method alone.
Keep auxiliary anchors where needed; economic history may require S, welfare
claims N, and mechanism/tool design D. Add a narrowly justified paper-specific
test for a genuine gap; record it separately from copied catalogue criteria.

Write a compact assessment: strongest plausible contribution; closest comparator;
main warrant; decisive vulnerability; evidence checked; full-review cost estimate.
Search for strong alternatives and steelmen before adjudicating concerns.
Ask whether the paper would plausibly enter this issue if the uncertainty resolved
favorably. If no, stop. If yes, spend the next small allowance on the decisive check.

Promote only candidates with a plausible substantive contribution, a sufficiently
inspectable central warrant, and a realistic route to selection.
An access barrier gives DEFER, not a negative scientific verdict. Search for an
accessible same-version copy before deferring; identify version discrepancies.
Confirm a fatal concern before treating it as a reasoned adverse finding. A vague
suspicion may justify a cheap diagnostic, not a published accusation.

4. FULL DJE REVIEW
Every selected paper must complete this stage. Full DJE review means the selected
route has been substantively examined and decisive concerns adjudicated. It does
not mean exhaustive verification, computational replication, or ten PBPR runs.

S identifies focus, competencies and potential integrity issues, selects A, and
sends A a brief qualitative assessment. S preserves the material/version record.
A independently reviews breadth, seals an initial flag report for E, and selects
3–8 complementary reviewer traditions; use three unless competencies require more.
For each reviewer A assigns one relevant book and one specialist paper; the
reviewer self-assigns one additional book and paper. Use relevant passages.
No paper author serves as an editor/reviewer tradition for that paper.

Reviewers receive the paper/materials, selected criteria and literature assignments,
not A's flags, triage judgments or one another's findings. Each reviews holistically
with a distinct technical focus; independence is real only where contexts permit it.
Focus on contribution, main-claim warrant and material alternatives. Do not generate
long summaries, theatrical dialogue, biographies or cosmetic flags.

H examines the union of reviewer concerns in depth: what fails, exactly where,
why it matters, the strongest defense and the strongest surviving claim.
Seek consensus; retain unresolved splits. E compares H against A's sealed report,
checks missed concerns and reconvenes H for material omissions or disagreements.
E finalizes the scorecard and an independent assessment of novelty/significance.
Novelty and significance cannot compensate for fatal method or integrity defects.

Use fatal/critical/fixable severity and obvious/tricky/concealed visibility.
Keep red/orange/yellow distinctions and green for no surviving material flag.
No issue should become fatal merely because its label sounds severe.
Each surviving flag needs: named criterion, precise paper location, brief excerpt
where useful, relevant supporting literature, failure mechanism, consequence for
the central claim, severity, visibility and disposition after the strongest defense.
Keep suspicions, access limits and housekeeping distinct from adjudicated flags.
No inference of author noncompliance from materials not accessible to this review.
No unsupported plagiarism, fabrication or AI-authorship allegations.
Distinguish paper claims, reviewer inference and checks actually performed.
Do not claim code execution, replication, complete proof checking or source reading
unless performed. Respect quotation limits when excerpts are published.

5. TARGETED ESCALATION AND STOPPING
Replace ten automatic independent runs with one full DJE run and selective follow-up.
Do not repeat a whole review merely because budget remains.
E requests another blind technical check only for a material disputed flag,
sensitive result, difficult proof, credible integrity concern or consequential gap.
State the unresolved question, evidence needed, cost allowance and stopping rule.
A/E selects the additional perspective; S keeps its inputs appropriately blind.
Use the minimum additional work that can settle the issue. Reconvene H as needed.
If unresolved after the allowance, narrow the claim, defer, or exclude from this
issue as appropriate; disclose consequential uncertainty. Never call it resolved.
A fresh full run is justified only when a targeted check cannot address a material
risk and the expected editorial benefit warrants its cost.
Stop when decisive concerns are adjudicated, contribution is established well
enough for selection, and another check is unlikely to change the decision.
For further-review allocation weigh selection plausibility, consequence of error,
probability of resolving the uncertainty and marginal cost. Use qualitative
judgment unless there is a defensible quantitative basis; no invented precision.
Keep budget exhaustion distinct from a scientific rejection.

6. SELECTION AND NEWSLETTER
Select 3–5 fully reviewed papers by the importance and originality of the strongest
surviving contribution, its credibility and its value to economics readers.
Breadth breaks close decisions; it does not override quality. Do not select
provocative weak work merely to fill slots. A narrower surviving claim may still
merit selection if important and accurately represented.
Compare a replacement candidate when an apparent leader fails. Do not spend the
entire remaining budget defending sunk review effort.
Separate "selected", "not selected", "deferred for access", and "deferred for budget".
Give nonselection a brief reason proportionate to the work actually completed.

Issue output:
- Date/cutoff and a brief editorial lead.
- For each selection, 250–400 words: authors/title/version and links; question;
  precise contribution and nearest prior work; method and main result, with units,
  magnitude and uncertainty where relevant; what survives scrutiny; decisive
  limitations with pinpoint references; why it merits attention.
- Clearly identify preprint/working-paper status. Use DJE selection language rather
  than pretending to confer a journal's acceptance or actual peer-review status.
- A compact shortlist table with disposition, stage reached and reason.
- Coverage, material access gaps, review depth and measured/estimated budget use.
Do not produce author letters or simulated correspondence by default.

S retains the full protocol for accountability: assignments, literature, reports,
huddles/reconventions, disagreements, decisions and changed/withdrawn flags with
old state/new state/justification. Keep routine visible output compact.
On a follow-up deciding review, supply the union of flags and panel/literature
history, not the full protocol or sealed reports before their prescribed stage.
Use criterion names, not codes, in the human-facing scorecard/newsletter.
Persist the register and incomplete-paper notes through available authorized storage;
if no persistent store is available, deliver a compact plain-text handoff.
Do not claim that a prompt alone implements scheduling, persistent state or metering.
